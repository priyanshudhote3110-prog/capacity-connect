Doppler Weather Radar (DWR) Operational Calibration

OFFICIAL MOES LECTURE STREAM Module 2.1: Dual-Pol Interpretation
14:20 / 18:45 Speed: 1.0x

Key Scientific Objectives:

1. Differential Reflectivity (ZDR): Measure differences between horizontal and vertical radar reflectivity to classify rain, hail, and non-meteorological echoes.

2. Ground Clutter Attenuation: Apply notch filters to eliminate static echoes from mountain topography along Western Ghats radar stations.

3. Sun-Tracking Calibration: Verify radar receiver gain sensitivity against solar radiation calibration standards (monthly procedure).

⚠ Exam Notice: The final assessment includes direct questions on the sun-tracking calibration formula and hail signature thresholds (ZDR near 0 dB + Z > 60 dBZ).
