## 1. Getting Started & Authentication

### Role-Prefixed Identifiers
Capacity Connect automatically assigns official enterprise identification codes based on role:
- **`ADM-xxx`**: Ministry Executive Leadership & System Administrators
- **`TRN-xxx`**: Accredited Training Faculty & Subject Matter Experts
- **`EMP-xxx`**: Scientists, Technical Officers & Operational Learners

### Login Options
1. **Google Sign-In:** Click **Sign In with Google** to authenticate with your official or institutional Google account.
2. **Phone Number + OTP:** Enter your 10-digit mobile number, click **Request OTP via SMS**, and enter the 6-digit verification code.
3. **Quick Demo Personas (For Judges & Evaluators):** In the Sign-In modal, click any 1-click persona:
   - `ADM-001`: Dr. M. Ravichandran (Secretary MoES)
   - `TRN-001`: Dr. Anita Desai (Chief Radar Specialist & Faculty Head)

## 2. Learner Workflow (Scientists & Technical Staff)

1. **Profile Setup:**
   - Click your profile chip in the top navigation bar.
   - Upload your circular profile photo.
   - Select your MoES Institute (`IMD`, `INCOIS`, `IITM`, `NCMRWF`, `NIOT`, `NCPOR`).
   - Fill in designation, department, and specialization.

2. **Browsing & Enrolling in Courses:**
   - Go to **Courses & Playlists**.
   - Filter courses by Institute or Search keyword.
   - Click **Start Learning** on any module.

3. **Video Classroom & Study Notes:**
   - Watch lessons at speeds from `0.5x` up to `2.0x`.
   - Take timestamped personal study notes while watching.
   - Seek directly to specific video moments by clicking timestamp badges.

4. **Live Classrooms:**
   - When a session has the pulsing **LIVE NOW** badge, click **Join Live Session**.
   - Chat in real-time with the faculty.
   - Use **Raise Hand** to notify the instructor.
   - Participate in interactive live polls.

5. **Formal Certification Exam:**
   - Click **Take Certification Exam** from any completed course.
   - Answer questions using the timed test interface and interactive Question Palette.
   - Submit the exam to receive an instant diagnostic topic scorecard.
   - If you score **≥ 70%**, your official Government of India Certificate is issued immediately!

6. **Viewing & Printing Certificates:**
   - Access **Certificates** from the navigation bar.
   - View your tamper-proof certificate with dual guilloche gold border, Ashok Stambh emblem, SHA-256 hash, and scannable QR code.
   - Click **Print Certificate** or **Download PDF**.

## 3. Faculty & Trainer Workflow

1. **Scheduling a Live Masterclass:**
   - Navigate to **Live Classroom**.
   - Click **➕ Schedule New Session**.
   - Enter session title, select linked course, and set start time.

2. **Moderating Live Sessions:**
   - Monitor real-time chat questions.
   - View attendee count and raised hands.

3. **Doubt Resolution:**
   - Visit **Doubt Forum**.
   - Review unanswered technical questions from learners across the 6 institutes.
   - Post answers marked with the green **Verified Faculty Solution** badge.

---

## 4. Ministry Executive & Admin Workflow

1. **Executive Skill-Gap Heatmap:**
   - Navigate to **Skill Heatmap**.
   - Review cross-institutional scores across Doppler Radar, HPC Slurm, Ocean Telemetry, Deep-Sea Robotics, Polar Safety, and GeM Compliance.
   - Pinpoint critical gaps highlighted in red (< 50%).

2. **1-Click Mandatory Training Order:**
   - Click **⚡ Mandate Cohort** next to any deficit competency.
   - Confirm the prompt to issue a Ministry Executive Directive with a compliance deadline.
   - Automatically dispatches compliance alerts to all institute personnel.

3. **Compliance Reporting:**
   - Click **📥 Export Compliance CSV** to download complete certification and enrollment data for MoES annual reporting.
